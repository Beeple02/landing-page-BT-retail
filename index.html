FAQ

Questions.

What exchanges does it cover?
NER (17 securities total) and TSE. Both exchanges are live with real-time prices, order books, and full trade history.
Is the data actually live?
Yes. Prices update in real-time via a live SSE stream. The order book refreshes every few seconds. The ticker tape scrolls live market data.
What currency is the pricing in?
Redmont Dollars (R$). The terminal also supports Alexandrian Pounds (A£) at the standard 10:1 rate in the Enterprise settings.
What's the difference between Retail and Enterprise?
Retail gives you the full trading terminal — all 14 pages, live data, analytics, backtesting. Enterprise adds portfolio management for multiple clients, a firm dashboard, morning brief, and institutional reporting.
Can I try it before subscribing?
There's a public demo version with live read-only data — you can see everything, but trading is disabled. Ask for the demo link when you get in touch.
